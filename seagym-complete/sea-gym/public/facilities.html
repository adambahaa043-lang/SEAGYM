Facilities

A practical training floor organised for strength work and conditioning.

Free-weight floor

Multiple squat racks, benches, and platforms. Bumper plates and calibrated plates available. Designed for barbell work without constant waiting.

Dumbbells & machines

Full dumbbell range, cable stations, and a selection of selectorised machines for accessory work and rehabilitation-friendly loading.

Conditioning zone

Concept2 rowers, assault bikes, ski erg, sleds, and open space for carries and metabolic circuits.

Mobility & recovery

Dedicated mats, foam rollers, bands, and a quiet corner for warm-up and cool-down work.

Changing rooms and showers are available on site. Lockers are provided for members during their session.
